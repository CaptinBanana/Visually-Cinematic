# Visually Cinematic (Fabric 26.3)

[![Fabric](https://img.shields.io/badge/Loader-Fabric%2026.3-blue?style=flat-square)](https://fabricmc.net/)
[![Modrinth](https://img.shields.io/badge/Modrinth-Visually%20Cinematic-1bd96a?style=flat-square)](https://modrinth.com/modpack/visuallycinematic)

</div>

**Visually Cinematic** is a curated Vanilla+ survival modpack designed to make Minecraft feel like a living, breathing film — without stripping away the charm or progression of the base game. 

Rather than burying you in bloated tech trees or jarring mechanics, this pack focuses on **atmosphere, motion, soundscape, and scale**. Every mod is chosen to work in harmony so that simply walking through a forest, sailing across an ocean, or taking shelter from a thunderstorm feels tactile, grounded, and genuinely cinematic.

---

## 🎬 What Makes It Cinematic?

### Camera Craft & Fluid Motion
Movement in vanilla Minecraft can feel rigid. **Visually Cinematic** reimagines how you see and move through the world :
* **Dynamic Perspective :** **Camera Overhaul** adds subtle banking, pitch, and momentum to your view, while **First-person Model** lets you see your own body, armour, and actions in real time.
* **Tactile Combat & Interactions :** **Punchy!** (paired with **Punchy! Moves** and **Hyper Punchy**) gives swinging, blocking, and moving weighty, expressive first-person animations.
* **Cinematic Flight :** **Do a Barrel Roll** transforms Elytra gliding into full 360-degree aerial flight, complete with wingtip trails from **Elytra Contrails**.
* **Built-In Filming & Capture Tools :** Smooth out distant vistas with **Cinematic Zoom**, kick back and let **Ji AFK Cinematic** pan across your builds while you idle, or capture full scenes and screenshots with **Flashback**, **Freecam**, and **Renice Shot**.

### A Living, Breathing Atmosphere
The world reacts to weather, light, and movement at every scale :
* **Lighting & Skies :** Driven by **Iris Shaders** with **Complementary Shaders – Reimagined**, paired with volumetric skies from **Better Clouds** and real-time handheld glow from **LambDynamicLights**.
* **Reactive Weather & Particles :** Rain isn't just a flat overlay — **Particle Rain**, **Cool Rain**, and **Immersive Storms** bring drifting mist, splashes, and heavy downpours. Indoors and underground, you'll spot drifting spores via **Cave Dust Rethinking**, swaying **Swinging Lanterns**, rising **Better Smoke**, and **Rustling Leaves**.
* **Expressive Mobs & Details :** **Fresh Animations** brings every mob to life with fluid movement, **Wakes** leaves trailing ripples behind boats and swimmers, and **Subtle Effects**, **Visuality**, and **Fancy World Animations** add micro-details that make the world feel cinematic.

### Acoustic Depth
Visuals are only half the immersion. **Sound Physics Remastered** introduces realistic reverberation and sound occlusion — caves echo, forests dampen distant noise, and stone halls carry footsteps. Paired with **AmbientSounds**, **Presence Footsteps**, and **ImmersiveThunder : Reforged**, every biome has its own distinct audio identity.

## 🌍 A World Worth Exploring

* **Grand-Scale Terrain :** **Tectonic**, **Terralith**, **Continents**, and **Streams Reflowing** reshape the Overworld into sweeping mountain ranges, flowing rivers, and vast oceans separating distinct landmasses.
* **Overhauled Dimensions :** **Incendium Legacy** and **Nullscape** bring the same sense of scale and eerie beauty to the Nether and the End.
* **Rich Structures & Wildlife :** Explore overhauled vanilla landmarks via **Dungeons and Taverns** (including all 10 major structure overhauls), discover atmospheric settlements from **Towns and Towers**, **Tidal Towns**, and **Structory**, and encounter new fauna with **Naturalist** and **Variants&Ventures**.

---

## 🏕️ Grounded Vanilla+ Survival

Gameplay additions stay faithful to vanilla progression while rewarding exploration and settlement building :
* **Cosy Cooking & Living :** **Farmer's Delight Refabricated** — expanded with **Brewin' And Chewin' Fly**, **Rustic Delight**, **My Nether's Delight**, **Display Delight**, **Hands-On Delight**, and **Storage Delight** — turns food into a rewarding, placeable, interactive part of base life.
* **Smarter Exploration :** Navigate with **Diorama**, **Nature's Compass**, and **Explorer's Compass**, and expand your carry capacity naturally using **Traveler's Backpack**, **Bundle Upgrade**, and **Easy Shulker Boxes**.
* **Polished, Uncluttered UI :** **EclipseUI**, **Immersive Hotbar**, **Dynamic Crosshair**, **Blur+**, **Jade**, and **JEI** keep information accessible without cluttering your screen.

---

## ⚡ Engineered for Smoothness

Heavy visuals shouldn't mean choppy frame rates. Under the hood, the pack runs on a modern Fabric optimisation stack — including **Sodium**, **Lithium**, **C2ME**, **ScalableLux**, **FerriteCore**, **ModernFix-mVUS**, **ImmediatelyFast**, **Async**, **AsyncParticles**, and **Entity/More Culling** — to keep chunk loading snappy and frame pacing consistent.

> **Recommended RAM :** Allocate **4 GB – 6 GB** in your launcher settings (avoid exceeding 8 GB to prevent garbage collection stutter).
