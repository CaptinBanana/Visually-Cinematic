# Visually Cinematic

**Visually Cinematic** is a Vanilla+ Fabric 26.3 modpack designed to turn every world into a theatrical experience - without losing the soul of the base game. Blending panoramic continental landscapes and light survival additions with dramatic shader lighting, fluid camera motion, layered animations, and immersive soundscapes, it frames your adventures like a living film while keeping frame rates high and quality-of-like tools seamlessly out of your way.

---

## 🌍 World Generation & Vanilla+ Survival

* **Overhauled Terrain:** [Terralith](https://modrinth.com/mod/terralith), [Tectonic](https://modrinth.com/mod/tectonic), [Continents](https://modrinth.com/mod/continents), and [Streams Reflowing](https://modrinth.com/mod/streams-reflowing) reshape the world into sweeping landmasses, towering mountain ranges, flowing rivers, and atmospheric biomes built with vanilla blocks.
* **Cozy Survival Additions:** [Farmer's Delight Refabricated](https://modrinth.com/mod/farmers-delight-refabricated) expands farming and cooking, [Traveler's Backpack](https://modrinth.com/mod/travelersbackpack) adds upgradeable wearable packs, and [Crops Love Rain](https://modrinth.com/mod/crops-love-rain) lets rainfall hydrate and nourish crops.
* **Dynamic Flight & Tweaks:** [Do a Barrel Roll](https://modrinth.com/mod/do-a-barrel-roll) and [Elytra Contrails Mod](https://modrinth.com/mod/elytra-contrails-mod) overhaul elytra flight, joined by [NetherPortalFix](https://modrinth.com/mod/netherportalfix), [Trade Cycling](https://modrinth.com/mod/trade-cycling), and [The Block Keeps Ticking](https://modrinth.com/mod/the-block-keeps-ticking).

## 🛠️ Pre-Configured Out of the Box

* **Automatic Resource Pack Order:** Uses [Packed Packs](https://modrinth.com/mod/packed-packs) so all 19 bundled resource packs load in the exact intended layer order on first launch.
* **Conflict-Free Mod Keybinds:** Uses [Default Options](https://modrinth.com/mod/default-options) to resolve overlapping mod controls out of the box without overwriting your personal **Modrinth Synced Game Options**.
* **Update-Safe Configs:** Uses [Config Manager](https://modrinth.com/mod/configmanager) so curated mod and shader defaults apply on a fresh install while keeping your own in-game tweaks intact across pack updates.

## ⚙️ Recommended Setup

* **Loader:** Fabric (`26.3`)
* **Environment:** Singleplayer, LAN, or Multiplayer *(must be installed on both client and server)*
* **Allocated RAM:** `4 GB – 6 GB` recommended *(avoid allocating more than 8 GB to prevent garbage-collection lag spikes)*
* **Shaders:** Enabled by default - toggle anytime via **Video Settings → Shader Packs** (or with your shader toggle keybind).
* **Mod Configs:** Adjust mod settings and configs to your preference via **Mod Menu**.
